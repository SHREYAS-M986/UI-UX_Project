### Standard SUS Scoring Formula
1. For odd-numbered items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): Score contribution = $\text{User Response} - 1$.
2. For even-numbered items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): Score contribution = $5 - \text{User Response}$.
3. Multiply the sum of the item score contributions by $2.5$ to obtain the overall composite SUS score ($0–100$).
- Benchmark Interpretation:
  - $\ge 80.3$: Grade A (Top 10% usability)
  - $68.0–80.2$: Grade B / Average (Acceptable)
  - $< 68.0$: Below average (Usability concerns present)
