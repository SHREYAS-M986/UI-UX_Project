# Informed Consent Agreement for User Research & Usability Testing
**Project Title**: AccessAudit — Accessibility Audit Tool for Developers  
**Institution**: Department of Computer Science & Engineering, East Point College of Engineering & Technology  
**Faculty Guide**: Prasanth, Assistant Professor, Dept. of CSE  

---

### Purpose of the Study
The purpose of this academic research study is to understand how software engineers and designers interact with accessibility audit interfaces, evaluate automated WCAG defect prioritization mechanisms, and test the usability of the AccessAudit prototype dashboard.

### Participation & Procedures
- You will participate in an interview and/or hands-on usability testing session lasting approximately 20–35 minutes.
- You will be asked to perform routine accessibility triage tasks on a web dashboard and provide think-aloud feedback.
- We are testing the usability of the tool, **not your personal skills or technical capability**. There are no right or wrong answers.

### Confidentiality & Data Management
- All data collected—including interview notes, task completion times, error counts, and survey ratings—will be kept strictly confidential and used solely for academic research and report documentation.
- Quotes and observations may be cited in the academic project report anonymously using participant identifiers (e.g., Participant P-01).

### Voluntary Participation
- Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may decline to answer any question or terminate the session at any time without penalty.
